SURVEILLANCE SUMMARY

Following the disappearance and eventual recovery of Subject B-001, all available surveillance recordings connected to the investigation were retrieved from archived storage.

During the original investigation, no unusual activity was reported by investigators reviewing the camera footage.

Years later, advances in forensic image enhancement allowed the recordings to be examined again. Multiple visual anomalies were identified that had not been documented in the original case.

No evidence of digital manipulation, camera malfunction, or unauthorized editing was discovered. The origin of the anomalies remains unknown.
